# Thea Energy

> Thea Energy, Inc. is a fusion energy company in Kearny, New Jersey, founded in 2022 out of
> Princeton University and the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory. It is reinventing the
> stellarator using a phased array of simple planar electromagnetic coils in place of complex
> 3D modular coils, moving field-shaping complexity from hardware into a software control
> stack. Its first integrated machine is Eos; the follow-on electricity-producing plant is
> Helios.

## Open source

Thea Energy's only public software surface. These are nuclear-engineering and simulation
libraries, not API client SDKs.

- [Thea-Energy on GitHub](https://github.com/Thea-Energy): Organization, 7 public repositories.
- [basalt](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/basalt): Nuclear meshing library for NX and Parasolid based on the Simmetrix mesh suite (MIT). Published to PyPI as [basalt-mesh](https://pypi.org/project/basalt-mesh/); docs at [basalt-mesh.readthedocs.io](https://basalt-mesh.readthedocs.io).
- [stellarvista](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/stellarvista): 3D visualizations for DAGMC and OpenMC (MIT).
- [moab-python](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/moab-python): Python work around the MOAB mesh-oriented database.
- [aws-builds](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/aws-builds): Build scripts for the simulation toolchain on AWS.
- Forks of upstream codes: [openmc](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/openmc), [opensn](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/opensn), [TMAP8](https://github.com/Thea-Energy/TMAP8).
